# CCPL — Official Tournament Format (20 Teams)

**User-defined fair format · 21 matches · 4 days**

---

## Flow

```
20 teams
   ↓  Round 1: 10 matches
10 WINNERS + 10 losers
   ↓
Top 4 winners ──────────────────────→ QF directly (seeds 1–4)
   ↓
6 remaining winners + 2 best losers (NRR)
   = 8 teams
   ↓  Play-off: 4 matches (8 → 4)
4 winners
   ↓
QF: 4 (bye) + 4 (play-off) = 8 teams → 4 matches
   ↓
SF: 2 matches → Final: 1
```

---

## Stage 1 — Round 1 (10 matches · **5 overs**)

- 20 teams · random draw · 10 fixtures · **5 overs per side**
- **Win = 2 pts** · Loss = 0 · Tie/NR = 1
- Result: **10 winners**, **10 losers**

---

## Stage 2 — Who goes where

| Path | Teams | How selected |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Direct QF** | **4** | Top 4 among **10 winners** (points → NRR) |
| **Play-off** | **8** | Other **6 winners** + **2 best losers** (NRR) |

---

## Stage 3 — Play-off (8 teams → 4 matches → 4 winners · **5 overs**)

| Match | Pairing |
|-------|---------|
| PO-1 | Seed 5 vs Seed 12 |
| PO-2 | Seed 6 vs Seed 11 |
| PO-3 | Seed 7 vs Seed 10 |
| PO-4 | Seed 8 vs Seed 9 |

*Rank all 12 (top 4 + play-off 8) by Round 1 performance to assign seeds 5–12*

**4 winners** join the **4 direct QF** teams.

---

## Stage 4 — Quarter-finals (4 matches · **6 overs**)

| Match | Team 1 | Team 2 |
|-------|--------|--------|
| QF1 | Seed 1 | PO-4 winner |
| QF2 | Seed 2 | PO-3 winner |
| QF3 | Seed 3 | PO-2 winner |
| QF4 | Seed 4 | PO-1 winner |

---

## Stage 5 — Semi-finals + Final (**8 overs**)

| Match | Team 1 | Team 2 |
|-------|--------|--------|
| SF1 | QF1 winner | QF4 winner |
| SF2 | QF2 winner | QF3 winner |
| **FINAL** | SF1 winner | SF2 winner |

## Fairness

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Round 1 | All 20 play once — equal start |
| Top 4 | Earn QF bye by **best result + NRR** |
| Wildcards | **2 losers** with best NRR get **second chance** |
| Winners | All **10 winners** stay alive after R1 (6 in play-off, 4 direct QF) |
| No injustice | No winner eliminated after Round 1 |
