# APEX UX Prompt

Design for calm, professional decision support.

## Screen

**Name:**  
**Primary question (exactly one):**  

## States

Loading · Empty · Error · Offline · Success

## Recommendation block

Follow fixed order: Current View → Why → Evidence → Trade-offs → Risks → What Could Change → Suggested Next Step → Help Me Understand

## Explanation levels

L1 simple · L2 business · L3 metrics (user chooses depth)

## Anti-patterns (reject)

Engagement hacks · Urgency pressure · Multiple competing CTAs · Sensational language · Color-only risk signals

## Acceptance

Copy review · Touch targets · Motion purpose · Chart narrative summary
