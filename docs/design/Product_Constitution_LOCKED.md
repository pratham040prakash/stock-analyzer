# Product Constitution — LOCKED

**Product:** AI Trading Decision System  
**Status:** APPROVED · LOCKED  
**Revision policy:** No new partner surfaces, navigation redesign, or architecture changes without an explicit Product Constitution revision.

---

## Mission (frozen)

> Help traders consistently make better decisions that improve long-term profitability.

The AI performs the analysis. The UI communicates the conclusion. The user makes the decision.

**Gate:** Does this help the user make a better trading decision? If no → do not build.

---

## Architecture (frozen)

Exactly six partner surfaces:

1. **Today** — command center  
2. **Trades** — execution only  
3. **You** — relationship  
4. **Ask** — one-shot doubt  
5. **Trust** — accountability  
6. **Proof** — evidence  

No seventh surface. No alternate navigation. No additional product shells.

---

## Today is the product

Today must answer without exploration:

- Should I trade?
- What deserves my attention?
- What blocks me?
- What should I do next?

Verdict remains hero. Supporting intelligence below — prose-first only. No dashboards, metric grids, cards, charts, or long reports.

---

## Surface charters

| Surface | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Trades** | Entry · Stop · Target · Risk · Timing · Open in Kite · Proof |
| **You** | Trader state · behaviour insight · one portfolio action · one improvement |
| **Ask** | One question · one answer · no threads |
| **Trust** | Honest accountability · not analytics |
| **Proof** | AI-annotated evidence · not a charting workspace |

---

## Depth philosophy

Partner shell delivers decisions. Legacy tabs deliver understanding. Depth never competes with Today.

---

## Implementation order

| Phase | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| **P0** | Today Command Center |
| **P1** | Trades completion |
| **P2** | You, Ask, Trust, Proof refinement |
| **P3** | Engine-to-surface mapping audit |

No implementation may skip P0.

---

## Engine rule

Surface existing engine conclusions. Do not recompute duplicate intelligence.

---

## UX rule

Optimize for **confidence**, not minimalism or information density.

---

## Success metric

Within one minute the trader either executes one high-quality trade or confidently decides not to trade — without opening multiple screens.

---

## North star

TradingView explains markets. Brokers execute orders. News explains events. **This product tells the trader what to do.**
